How It Works

From reporting to resolution in four simple steps. Making civic engagement easier and more rewarding than ever.

1

Report the Issue

Take a photo, add your location automatically via GPS, and describe the civic issue you've spotted.

2

Auto-Routing

Our system automatically routes your report to the relevant municipal department based on location and issue type.

3

Track Progress

Receive real-time notifications as your issue moves through: Reported → Acknowledged → In Progress → Resolved.

Earn Rewards

Get points for reporting issues and helping your community. Climb the leaderboard and unlock rewards!
